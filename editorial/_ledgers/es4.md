## Reproduction (Merck owner page, Davidson AP, UC Davis; full review June 2018, updated June 2026) https://www.merckvetmanual.com/dog-owners/reproductive-disorders-of-dogs/management-of-reproduction-in-dogs
- Proestrus range 2 days-3 weeks; estrus range 3 days-3 weeks; discharge may turn straw coloured in estrus; vulva soft and "wrinkly" near estrus.
- Diestrus in non-pregnant dogs about 2 months.
- Pregnancy detectable by palpation ~day 21; ultrasound reliable by 25-35 days; relaxin blood test by day 30-35; fetuses visible on X-ray after ~day 45; X-rays after day 55 best for litter size.
- Rectal temperature drop usually precedes delivery by about 8-24 h. Stage I labour 12-24 h (restless, nesting, refusing food, panting, trembling, clear discharge).
- Dystocia criteria: stage I >24 h, stage II >12-24 h, or >1-4 h between puppies; dead/near-dead puppies or excessive maternal distress.

## VCA, Estrous Cycles in Dogs (Weir, Llera, Yuill; updated 13 Jun 2025) https://www.vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/estrus-cycles-in-dogs
- Puberty on average ~6 months, varies by breed; smaller breeds earlier.
- Small breeds may cycle three times a year.
- Can become pregnant on the very first cycle and at any point in estrus; "There are no valid reasons for letting a dog have a litter of puppies before she is spayed."
- Most vets recommend spaying before 6-7 months; some large breeds may benefit from delaying.

## VCA, Pyometra in Dogs (Barnes, Weir, Ward; updated 15 May 2026) https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/pyometra-in-dogs
- Life-threatening uterine infection after heat; typically 4-6 weeks after heat, range 2-16 weeks; more common in older dogs.
- Open (pus discharge) vs closed (swollen belly, severe illness fast); increased drinking/urination; vomiting/diarrhoea possible.
- Preferred treatment: surgical removal of uterus and ovaries; can be fatal without prompt treatment.

## VCA, Eclampsia in Dogs (Weir, Williams, Llera, Ward; updated 13 Jun 2025) https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/eclampsia-in-dogs
- Emergency drop in blood calcium; most often when puppies are 1-4 weeks old; can occur before birth.
- Early signs restlessness, panting, stiff movement; can progress to spasms and convulsions. Small breeds at higher risk.
- Calcium supplementation or dairy in pregnant/nursing dogs generally not recommended (can raise risk).

## Clinician's Brief summary of Evans KM, Adams VJ. J Small Anim Pract 2010;51:113-118 https://www.cliniciansbrief.com/column/category/column/capsules/c-section-cross-section
- 13,141 bitches, 22,005 litters, 151 breeds analysed (UK Kennel Club survey); caesarean rates particularly high in Bulldogs, French Bulldogs and Boston Terriers. Percentages not shown on loaded page (not cited). Elective vs emergency not separated.

## AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines 2019, Life Stage Checklists https://www.aaha.org/resources/life-stage-canine-2019/life-stage-checklist/
- Senior: routinely evaluate cognitive dysfunction, anxiety; emphasise weight control and muscle condition; adapt home for mobility, vision, hearing; watch tolerance to heat, cold and darkness; monitor periodontal disease. Page gives no age cutoff for "senior".

## VCA, Cognitive Dysfunction Syndrome in Senior Dogs and Cats (Lindell, Horwitz, Landsberg; updated 7 Aug 2026) https://www.vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/behavior-counseling-senior-pet-cognitive-dysfunction
- Slowly progressive age-related decline; DISHAA signs (disorientation, interactions, sleep-wake, house soiling/learning/memory, activity, anxiety); arthritis or kidney disease can mimic; enrichment, exercise, antioxidant diets; selegiline licensed in North America.

## AAAAI, Pet Allergy (page dated 7/9/2026) https://www.aaaai.org/conditions-treatments/allergies/pet-allergy
- Dander can take six months or more to clear after a pet leaves; fur carries pollen/mould.
- Keep pets out of bedroom (~8 h/day there); HEPA air cleaners; remove carpets/rugs; brush pet outdoors by a non-allergic person.
- Antihistamines not ideal long-term; allergy shots effective long-term treatment; allergist testing best method.

## Vredegoor DW et al. J Allergy Clin Immunol 2012;130(4):904-909.e7, doi:10.1016/j.jaci.2012.05.013 (Utrecht repository abstract) https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/can-f-1-levels-in-hair-and-homes-of-different-dog-breeds-lack-of-/
- "Hypoallergenic" breeds (Labradoodle, Poodle, Spanish Water Dog, Airedale) had higher Can f 1 in hair/coat than controls (n=196 vs 160); no difference in airborne levels between breeds; "no evidence for the classification of certain dog breeds as being hypoallergenic".

## MSD Vet Manual, Behavior Problems of Dogs (Borns-Weil, Sept 2025) https://www.msdvetmanual.com/behavior/behavior-of-dogs/behavior-problems-of-dogs
- Fear is the most common motivation for aggression; pain an important risk factor; 28%-82% of behaviour cases showed pain signs (study of behaviourists' cases); rule out medical causes.
- Avoid confrontation/punishment; avoid triggers; basket muzzle useful; refer moderate/severe aggression to a board-certified behaviourist; "assert dominance" techniques increase fear.
- Attention-seeking barking and begging arise from inadvertent reinforcement.

## PDSA, Vet Q&A: Why do puppies cry at night? (25 Jan 2021) https://www.pdsa.org.uk/what-we-do/blog/vet-qa-why-do-puppies-cry-at-night
- Reasons: used to sleeping with littermates/mother, need a toilet break, waking alone in the dark.
- Bedtime routine (meal, toilet, play, last toilet); may sleep in same room; don't ignore crying in the first nights; check toilet first; minimal fuss, no play.

## AKC, French Bulldog DNA Tests and Recommended Health Tests (accessed 2026-11-03) https://www.akc.org/breeder-programs/dna/dna-resource-center/akc-dna-health/breed-specific-dna-testing-recommendations/french-bulldog-dna-tests/
