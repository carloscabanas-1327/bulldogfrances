## New facts verified (WebFetch, 2026-11-03)
- Bordetella bronchiseptica "also causes disease in cats" and has "infrequently" caused illness in people, "typically those with compromised immunity"; incubation about 6 days (range 2-10). — "Bordetellosis", Cornell University Riney Canine Health Center, updated Nov 20, 2025 — https://www.vet.cornell.edu/index%2Ephp/departments-centers-and-institutes/riney-canine-health-center/canine-health-information/bordetellosis
- Flea dirt test: "Placed in water or on a damp paper towel, the excrement dissolves, producing a reddish brown color." Parting the hair reveals flea excrement or moving fleas. Classic flea-allergy signs: crusted, itchy skin over the hips, base of the tail and thighs. "It is the newly emerged, unfed fleas that infest pets and bite people." — "Fleas of Dogs" (Dog Owners), Merck Veterinary Manual, Michael W. Dryden, reviewed Jun 2018, updated Sep 2024 — https://www.merckvetmanual.com/dog-owners/skin-disorders-of-dogs/fleas-of-dogs
- Fleas: humans can act as carriers, bringing fleas into the household and infesting unprotected pets; adults begin feeding about 5 minutes after finding a host; flea dirt = "reddish-black fecal pellets". — "Fleas in Dogs and Cats", Merck Veterinary Manual (prof.), C. Gentry, Apr 2026 — https://www.merckvetmanual.com/integumentary-system/fleas-and-flea-allergy-dermatitis/fleas-in-dogs-and-cats
- Adult fleas "jump onto cats, dogs, and even people in the search for a blood meal"; "There is no evidence of fleas developing resistance to insecticides"; pupae can survive many months in cocoons and adults emerge in large numbers when pets/owners return home (fleas after boarding are not necessarily from the facility). — "Flea Control in Dogs", VCA Animal Hospitals, Sep 2025 — https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/flea-control-in-dogs
