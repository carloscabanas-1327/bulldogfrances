- FCI standard: weight males 9–14 kg, females 8–13 kg; 500 g tolerance over the upper limit for a typical specimen; height males 27–35 cm, females 24–32 cm (±1 cm); size/weight outside limits = disqualifying. Tail "naturalmente corta", twisted/knotted/broken or relatively long tail accepted if it does not pass the hock; absent tail = disqualifying. Nose must be black; non-black nose and fully closed nostrils = disqualifying. Valid standard published 10.08.2023. — FCI Standard N° 101, Bulldog francés (ES PDF, doc dated 20.12.2024) — https://www.fci.be/Nomenclature/Standards/101g09-es.pdf
- Car interior can rise almost 20 °F (~11 °C) in 10 min and almost 30 °F (~17 °C) in 20 min; at 1 h can be >40 °F (~22 °C) above outside; table: 70 °F (21 °C) outside → ~89 °F (32 °C) at 10 min, ~113 °F (45 °C) at 60 min; cracking windows "had very little effect" / "makes no difference". — "Pets in vehicles", AVMA (no date) — https://avma.org/public/PetCare/Pages/pets-in-vehicles.aspx
- Corkscrew (screw/ingrown) tail is a vertebral malformation; most often in English bulldogs, pugs, Boston terriers and French bulldogs; deep tail fold → infection; signs itching, odor, chewing tail, scooting; regular cleaning of the fold, many dogs after every bowel movement; cleaning reduces but usually does not eliminate infections; medical management "lifelong commitment"; surgery (removal of some/all tail vertebrae, general anesthesia) "can be curative". — "Corkscrew Tails", VCA Canada, T. Hunter & C. Barnette, updated 24 Apr 2025 — https://vcacanada.com/know-your-pet/corkscrew-tails
- Tail fold intertrigo: inflammatory condition of skin creases; most common in English bulldogs, "infrequently seen in French bulldogs, pugs, and Boston terriers"; signs scooting, foul odor at tail base, fecal buildup; dorsal folds respond better to conservative care, ventral tail fold pyoderma often impossible to maintain long term with cleaning; surgery = caudectomy + fold removal. — "Tail Fold Intertrigo (Screw Tail)", MSPCA-Angell, M. M. Pavletic DVM DACVS, published 24 Feb 2026 — https://www.mspca.org/clinical/tail-fold-intertrigo-screw-tail/
- IVDD recurrence in surgically treated French Bulldogs: 127 dogs, recurrence 52.7% (67/127); 52.2% of recurrences within first 12 months (~27.6% of all dogs, "almost every fourth"); cervical 47% vs thoracolumbar 56.6%; dogs first affected at ≤3 years most prone to cervical recurrence. Authors at University of Bern Vetsuisse Faculty (Switzerland), NOT Finland. — Leu D, Forterre F, Stein V, Vidondo B, Acta Vet Scand 2023;65:3 — https://boris.unibe.ch/178356/1/s13028-023-00667-0.pdf
- Normal rectal temperature, dog: 37.5–39.2 °C (99.5–102.5 °F). — "Normal Rectal Temperature Ranges", Merck Veterinary Manual (table, no date) — https://merckvetmanual.com/multimedia/table/normal-rectal-temperature-ranges
