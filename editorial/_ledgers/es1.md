# Ledger es1: new facts verified 2026-11-03 (Spanish genetics/breed guides)

- FCI standard No. 101 (Bouledogue français), official valid standard published 10.08.2023: coat "Smooth coat, close, glossy and soft, without undercoat"; colour "fawn, brindled or not, with or without white spotting" (brindle, fawn, "pied", "fawn and white"); "The nose is always black, in all coat colours, never brown or blue". — FCI, Standard 101 (English PDF) — https://www.fci.be/Nomenclature/Standards/101g09-en.pdf
- FCI standard 101: "The all-white subjects provided the edge of eyelids and nose are black are admitted but not bred for, because of a risk of deafness." — same URL
- FCI standard 101, disqualifying faults include: "Colour not in accordance with what is prescribed in the standard, namely black, black with fawn markings (black and tan) and all dilutions of black with or without white spotting"; "Long, wired-haired or woolly coat"; "Deafness"; "Respiratory distress"; "Completely closed nostrils"; "Heterochrome eyes (wall eye)"; "Colour of nose other than black". Merle is not mentioned anywhere. — same URL
- FCI standard 101: nose "Black, broad, snubbed, with symmetrical and well opened nostrils"; muzzle "Short, broad"; severe fault "Muzzle too long or excessively short"; no numeric muzzle:skull ratio. Weight males 9–14 kg, females 8–13 kg. — same URL
- UC Davis VGL, Dilute (D locus/Blue): autosomal recessive; variants d1 (common), d2, d3 (rare); two copies needed; D/d carriers pass a variant to ~half of offspring, two carriers ~25% dilute puppies; d2 present at <1% in phenotypically dilute French Bulldogs and d1-d3 do not explain all dilution in the breed; page does not mention CDA. — UC Davis Veterinary Genetics Laboratory — https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/test/dilute-dog
