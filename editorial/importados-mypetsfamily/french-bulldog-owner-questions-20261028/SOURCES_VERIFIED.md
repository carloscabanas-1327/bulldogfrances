# Verified sources ledger (started 2026-11-03)

Facts below were checked against the source page on 2026-11-03 before being used in an article.

| Source | Facts used |
|---|---|
| FBDCA, Health Testing and Registries (frenchbulldogclub.org/health/testingregistries/) | CHIC requires hip (OFA or PennHIP), CAER eye exam by DACVO (annual recommended), patella, cardiac (auscultation or echo); microchip/tattoo ID. Recommended: tracheal hypoplasia, elbows, JHC/HSF4, RFGS, cystinuria type 3 (PennGen). |
| AKC, French Bulldog DNA Tests | Parent-club tests: patella, hip, cardiac ("echocardiogram recommended but not required"), ophthalmologist. |
| AKC, Official Standard (approved 1991) | Weight not to exceed 28 lb (DQ); color DQs: solid black, mouse, liver, black and tan, black and white, white with black; merle not mentioned; coat "moderately fine, brilliant, short and smooth". |
| AKC, Most Popular Breeds | French Bulldog No. 1 for 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. |
| OFA, CHIC Programs | "The CHIC number itself does not imply normal test results." |
| O'Neill et al. 2021, Canine Med Genet 8:13 | 2,781 Frenchies vs 21,850 others; higher odds for 20/43 disorders; BOAS OR 30.89; stenotic nares 42.14; aural discharge 14.40; skin fold dermatitis 11.18; "no longer ... 'typical' dog". |
| Cambridge BOAS, Recognition and Diagnosis | Signs; Frenchies with moderate-severe stenotic nares ~20x more likely to develop BOAS; 3-min trot test; grades 0-III. |
| Royal Kennel Club, RFGS | Joint RKC/Cambridge scheme; from 12 months; repeat every 2 years for breeding dogs. |
| Liu et al. 2017, PLoS ONE 12(8):e0181928 (abstract) | 604 dogs; stenotic nostrils associated with BOAS in all three breeds; NGR and NLR significant in French bulldogs. |
| Hall, Carter, O'Neill 2020, Sci Rep (RVC repository tables) | 390 HRI cases / 905,543 dogs; French Bulldog OR 5.31 vs Labrador; Bulldog 12.53; brachycephalic 2.05 vs mesocephalic. DOI not yet confirmed. |
| RVC news 2023 + "How to Cool Hot Dogs" PDF | Cool first, transport second; immersion or water + air movement; wet towels far less effective; "cool slowly" is a myth; 97% vs 43% survival (mild vs severe, earlier RVC research). |
| UC Davis VGL, Merle | PMEL/SILV SINE insertion; incomplete dominance; cryptic merle; double merle defects; breeding two merles discouraged. |
| UC Davis VGL, Coat Length | Five recessive FGF5 variants; L4 in French Bulldog; "Long hair is also known as Fluffy in the French Bulldog"; carrier x carrier = 25% long. |
| Strain et al. 2009, JVIM 23(2):282-286 | 153 merle dogs; single merle 2.7% uni / 0.9% bi deaf; double merle 10% uni / 15% bi. |
| Merck Vet Manual, Deafness in Animals | Congenital deafness linked to piebald/merle; unilateral deafness may go undetected without BAER. |
| Merck Vet Manual, Hereditary Alopecia and Hypotrichosis (Moriello, Carnevale, Feb 2025) | CDA: MLPH recessive; born normal, signs before 1 year in blue/fawn areas; managed not cured; cosmetic; biopsy. |
| French Bulldog Rescue Network, Fad Colors | Advocacy position: merle introduced via crossbreeding (cited as the group's position, not as a lab finding). |

Not yet verified (do not cite until checked): OFA breed statistics percentages; Packer 2015 craniofacial ratio figures; UC Davis dilute (D locus) page; any AKC statement that registration does not imply health.
