What does the first heat look like?

A female dog entering a heat cycle may develop vulvar swelling and vaginal discharge, along with changes in behavior or attention from male dogs.

The discharge and timing can vary; the first cycle does not follow one exact timetable for every French Bulldog.

When can pregnancy happen?

Pregnancy can occur during the fertile part of the cycle. Owners should not assume that bleeding ending means pregnancy is impossible.

Prevent direct access to intact males throughout the risk period; fences and ordinary walks are not reliable birth control.

Which signs require veterinary advice?

Call a vet for unusual illness, profound lethargy, pain, foul discharge, or bleeding that seems excessive or persists unexpectedly.

Do not mistake every discharge for normal heat. An unwell dog needs examination.

Should owners breed at the first heat?

A first heat does not demonstrate physical maturity or suitability for breeding. French Bulldogs have important respiratory and reproductive welfare considerations.

Discuss long-term reproductive plans with a veterinarian, including the merits and risks of sterilization for the individual dog.

How can the dog stay comfortable?

Offer a calm routine, hygiene support as needed, and attentive supervision. Do not use hormonal medicines or home remedies without veterinary direction.

Track the start and changes in the cycle so a veterinarian can assess abnormalities if they arise.
